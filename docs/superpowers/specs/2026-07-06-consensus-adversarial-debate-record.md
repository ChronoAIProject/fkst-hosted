# PHASE 1 — BLIND POSITIONS

## Seat 1: TELEOLOGY (blind) — verdict: approve

Verified preface: two load-bearing facts confirmed by direct inspection before writing: (1) `prior_round_digests` is validated in `core.lua:304` and populated upstream at `libraries/devloop/payloads/builders.lua:364`, but a full grep confirms it is never rendered into any prompt — a convergence round shows workers only the single `convergence_question` line (`core/prompt_rendering.lua:88-91`); (2) the meta-judge reads only 600-char digests (`core.lua:20`, `prompt_rendering.lua:52-65`) and is instructed to "Respond with exactly one line and no other text" (`prompts/meta_judge.lua:15`).

### ESSENCE

Strip the costume and ask what this machine is *for*. An autonomous software company must decide, without a human at the gate, whether a proposed framing or diff should proceed. The failure mode that makes this hard is not disagreement — it is **correlated plausibility**: instances of the same model family, fed the same context and the same "make it green" objective, converge together on the same wrong-but-plausible answer, and a wrong merge produces no error fact, no stall, no red CI. The repo's own competence doctrine names this precisely: consensus among clones is not an oracle.

So the essence of the design problem is: **manufacture independent epistemic evidence that no single model instance can manufacture alone, force that evidence into confrontation so that errors can be *refuted* rather than merely outvoted, and compress the confrontation into one decision from a bounded set — under a hard budget of minutes-per-codex-call, an at-least-once idempotent pipeline, a ~64KiB payload constitution, and adversarially untrusted input text.**

Three corollaries fall out of this telos before we look at any draft:

1. **Independence is for priors, not for criteria.** Correlated failure is broken by judges with different *ways of seeing*, not by partitioning the checklist so each judge owns one criterion. Partitioned criteria create seams that nobody owns — the blind spot moves to the boundary between angles.
2. **Disagreement is information only if arguments meet.** A blind re-vote is a fresh sample from the same correlated distribution; a rebuttal is a *conditional* sample given the peer's argument. Only the second can break a correlated error, because the judge (a weak, bounded arbiter) can verify a refutation far more cheaply than it can verify a claim. This is the entire logic of debate-as-oracle protocols, and of Delphi: structured rounds *with peer feedback*.
3. **Beauty is the criterion because of what the failure mode looks like.** Plausible-but-wrong patches are, empirically in this repo, *ugly* in a specific, enumerable way — a tuned proxy constant, a symptom branch, a narrative without a verified premise. The BEAUTY GATE's six smells are the operationalization; and the doctrine itself rules that beauty judgment cannot be self-graded (self-assessment is always gamed) — it must hang on the existing adversarial machinery. The consensus engine *is* that machinery. Seating beauty here is not aesthetics; it is the constitution's own placement instruction.

### IDEAL

Derive the design purely from the essence, step by step, and see what shape is forced:

**(a) Three whole-picture judges with different priors.** One judge is capturable by narrative; two deadlock; three give a tiebreak and — decisively — the six smells factor cleanly into three lenses: *teleology* (skipped-purpose, missing-inevitability), *parsimony* (magic numbers, symptom branches), *fidelity* (proxy-over-truth, narrative-over-verification). Three is not a magic number here; it is the rank of the smell space. Each judge owns the whole gate and is *seeded* with one lens — never restricted to it. Each must derive the problem's essence *before* engaging the proposal's own story, because anchoring on the proposal's narrative is itself smell #4. Each must name its own weakest assumption, because a debate needs handles, and a position that cannot name its weakest point has not been thought through.

**(b) Unanimity short-circuits.** Most proposals are uncontroversial; the expensive machinery must be reached only by genuine disagreement. The degenerate case of debate is agreement — this is not an optimization bolted on, it is the mechanism's own limit case. Cost: 3 spawns, same as today.

**(c) On disagreement, arguments must meet in full.** Each judge reads both peers' complete positions and must attack the peer's *essence-derivation* (the root, not the leaves), then explicitly update or defend with named reasons. The rebuttal contract demands *search* for a weak premise, not the *conclusion* that one exists: "name the weakest premise, or state with evidence why you find none." A contract that mandates disagreement manufactures performative objections — the mirror image of correlated agreement, and equally worthless to the arbiter.

**(d) A bounded arbiter reads the whole tape and authors the memory.** The synthesis judge reads the full P1+P2 transcript (in-memory, within one invocation — the payload constitution binds payloads, not invocations), may graft a globally better framing with per-element attribution (evidence-gated, auditable), and outputs: reached, or a *named essence-level disagreement*. Crucially, in the ideal the judge has a second duty: **author the round's durable memory** — a findings-of-fact record ("settled: X, by refutation of Y; open: Z") written to the converge marker surface, rehydrated by any next round via the existing content-fetch mechanism. Not the stenographer's tape (unboundable), not a mechanical 600-char truncation (constitutionally banned) — an *adjudicated distillation*, which is the only bounded object that preserves fidelity. Inter-round memory is what makes an outer round a continuation of an argument rather than a re-roll of dice.

**(e) The outer loop shrinks to almost nothing.** Once arguments actually meet inside one invocation, a second full round is justified only when synthesis names an essence-level disagreement that new evidence could resolve. The budget for that is small — one, perhaps two continuations — after which the honest terminal is "essence-stall, reconcile to blocked with WHY." An eight-round cap is derived for amnesiac re-rolls; it is not derived for debates with memory.

**(f) The two teloi of the engine are calibrated apart.** *Design search* (converge mode) asks "what is the best framing?" — there, ideal-first judgment is the point. *Gate* (PR review) asks "is this increment correct and safe?" — there, the ideal sketch is context, and the contract must say explicitly: good-enough-and-clean is approvable; ugliness must be *evidenced* against the six smells, never inferred from "this is not my ideal." A gate that rejects the good for not being the best manufactures fix-loop churn, which is its own liveness pathology. Same judges, same smells, different verdict semantics — exactly as `verdict_mode` already separates them (`core.lua:68-73`).

**(g) Trust boundaries hold at every text crossing.** Peer positions may echo injected instructions from the untrusted proposal body; the existing neutralizer (`prompt_rendering.lua:3-31`) must therefore wrap peer text at phase-2/3 boundaries too, and verdict extraction must remain the fail-closed unique-adjacent-pair discipline of `core.lua:405-474` applied to a bounded tail of a longer document. Fidelity to the threat model is part of fidelity to the essence.

### CRITIQUE

Judge the current system and the draft against that ideal.

**The current system fails its own telos at the exact point where the telos bites — and the draft's diagnosis is verified, not narrative.** Three facts, each checkable:

- Angles are *forbidden* whole-picture judgment: "State the reason that is specific to THIS angle; do not restate another angle's criterion" (`prompts/angle.lua:4`). This is partitioned-criteria voting, corollary (1) inverted: the seams belong to no one.
- The sole integrating mind reads three 600-char digests (`core.lua:20`, `prompt_rendering.lua:52-65`) and is budgeted **one line of output** (`prompts/meta_judge.lua:15`). At the moment maximal information is needed, the channel is narrowest.
- The convergence loop is **amnesiac**: `prior_round_digests` is validated (`core.lua:304`), faithfully built upstream (`builders.lua:364`), and never rendered into any prompt. A round-N+1 worker sees exactly one narrowed-question line (`prompt_rendering.lua:88-91`), often the near-contentless default "Angle verdicts: minimal=approve, structural=abstain…" (`core.lua:650-661`). Rounds are re-rolls, not arguments — which is why the loop historically ran to round 33 and why no-unanimity proposals stall. The system has been carrying the *input slot for memory* while never opening it: the machine's own body testifies to the missing organ.

Against this, the draft's core moves are genuinely **derived, not bolted on**: whole-picture judges with lens seeds (corollary 1); full peer text in rebuttal (corollary 2); full-transcript synthesis with attributed grafting (ideal d); unanimity fast path preserved (ideal b); beauty seated on the adversarial machinery per the constitution's own instruction (corollary 3); the lens trichotomy is the six-smell factorization, so even the number three passes the inevitability test. Cost is a purpose-improvement, not an add: 7 spawns per *informative* round versus up to 32 spawns of amnesiac re-rolling.

But the draft fails my ideal at four points, one of which is essential in kind though not in skeleton:

1. **Skipped teleology at the outer loop — the original sin survives one level up.** The draft declares the outer converge loop "unchanged (round cap ~8, true-stall reconcile)." Unchanged means: the transcript dies with the invocation, and round N+1 receives — what? If `prior_round_digests` remains unrendered, the debate fixes *intra-round* amnesia while preserving *inter-round* amnesia, and the outer loop remains a re-roll of an entire debate. What is an outer round *for*, once debate exists? It can only be the continuation of a named, unresolved essence disagreement — which requires the adjudicated findings of round N as input. The mechanism exists (`content_fetch` manifests, converge markers); the draft does not connect it. And the retained cap of ~8 is now an **inherited magic number**: derived for blind re-rolls, undefended for debates with memory, where the purpose-derived budget is 1–2 continuations then essence-stall.
2. **A narrative-driven rebuttal device.** "Each peer argument contains at least one weak premise — name it" mandates a conclusion, not a search. Under a contract that punishes "I find no flaw," judges will fabricate flaws to comply — performative disagreement, Goodhart's law applied to rebuttals, corrupting exactly the signal the arbiter needs (a *genuine* refutation is cheap to verify; a manufactured one is expensive noise). The fail-closed form is: *name the weakest premise, or state with evidence why none is found.*
3. **Unexamined fusion of the two teloi.** The phase-1 contract ("sketch the most beautiful solution, NOT constrained by the proposal") is the right forcing device for design search and a churn generator for the gate: in PR-review mode it invites rejecting a correct, clean increment for failing to be the reviewer's unconstrained ideal. The draft keeps `gate` mode and a fourth security seat (correctly derived from the untrusted-input axiom) but does not calibrate the ideal-sketch's *authority* per mode. The spec must say: in gate mode the ideal is context; only evidenced smells block.
4. **The trust boundary at peer-text crossings is unspecified.** Phase-2 workers ingest full phase-1 text that may second-hand-echo injected instructions from the proposal body; phase-3 ingests both. The existing neutralizer and unique-adjacent-pair parse discipline (`core.lua:405-474`) must be explicitly extended to these new crossings, and multi-section position papers need a defined fail-closed extraction contract. This is fidelity to the threat model, and it is absent from the draft as summarized.

None of these four demands a different skeleton. All four demand that the spec absorb them before implementation. Point 1 is the one I will die on: **a debate whose record does not survive the round is not a debate; it is theater with better acting.**

### VERDICT

approve

### WEAKEST

My argument assumes that a synthesis-authored findings-record can carry inter-round memory with adequate fidelity inside durable-surface bounds — if an adjudicated distillation loses the load-bearing detail of a refutation, the outer loop degrades back toward the re-roll I condemned, and my case for "approve with amendments" over "abstain" collapses.

## Seat 2: PARSIMONY (blind) — verdict: abstain

*All citations verified against the working tree at HEAD (d5545a7a).*

### ESSENCE

This design problem is not "make the judges debate." It is this: **in multi-judge consensus, the unit of value is the argument, not the verdict — and the current engine systematically destroys arguments at every boundary it owns.** Verified, boundary by boundary:

- The meta-judge never sees an argument. It sees 600-character digests (`max_digest_len = 600`, core.lua:20; `angle_digests`, core.lua:565-589), and sees each one **twice** — `render_angle_outputs` emits both `Reply:` and `Digest:` (core/prompt_rendering.lua:55-58), and `digest = reply` whenever the reply parsed (core.lua:574). The full angle stdout exists in memory (decide/main.lua:82) and is thrown away.
- The next round never sees the last round. A convergence re-round shows each worker exactly one added line — `Convergence question:\n<narrowed_question>` (core/prompt_rendering.lua:88-91). Nothing else survives.
- The one mechanism built to carry memory is dead cargo. `prior_round_digests` is written by devloop (libraries/devloop/payloads/builders.lua:364), validated by consensus (core.lua:234-247, enforced at core.lua:304), and **rendered into no prompt anywhere** — repo-wide grep confirms zero consumers. It is a freight car that is inspected at every station and unloaded at none.

So today's "convergence" is amnesiac re-sampling: up to 8 re-rolls (`max_converge_rounds() = 8`, libraries/devloop/config.lua:135) of blind dice with a one-sentence hint, where agreement can arrive by exhaustion or by chance rather than by any objection having been confronted. The quality a consensus engine actually owes us is: **the probability that the strongest objection to the proposal was confronted before approval.** Verdict-agreement over truncated digests is a proxy for that ground truth.

The essence of the redesign, therefore: **relocate deliberation to the only place where full arguments exist — in memory, inside one invocation — expose each position's weakest premise to an adversary who can see it, and force the synthesis to account for that confrontation**; all under the fixed physics: the ~64KiB reliable-payload boundary is a *lossy channel* and must never carry the debate itself; unanimity must stay at 3 spawns because each spawn is minutes of frontier compute; untrusted content must stay neutralized; every phase must be bounded and terminating.

Everything else — seat counts, phase counts, sentinel names — is surface, and every piece of surface must prove its right to exist.

### IDEAL

**1. One invocation = one complete deliberation; the outer loop demoted to a liveness shell.** Since the payload boundary is lossy by constitution (content never serialized into reliable payloads), any design that routes argument *through* it re-creates the amnesia it set out to cure. Therefore the debate must **complete** before crossing that boundary, and what crosses is a decision plus one named essence-level disagreement — never the debate. Corollary: if the inner debate works, an outer re-round is justified only by *new external facts*, and its recurrence should be near zero. The outer loop keeps its retry/stall/budget duties and loses its deliberative pretensions.

**2. The minimal adversarial structure, each element forced into existence:**
- *Blind positions* — because peer-invisibility is the only decorrelation the same-model family offers.
- *Confrontation, conditionally* — only when disagreement has proven the expense necessary (unanimity fast-path first, exactly today's 3 spawns). Confrontation cannot be delegated to the synthesis judge alone for two structural reasons: (a) self-named weakness is motivated reasoning — this repo's own doctrine that self-grading is always gamed (CLAUDE.md, BEAUTY GATE: 自评丑必被作弊) applies to a philosopher's ⟦WEAKEST⟧ exactly as to an author's self-review; (b) a judge who is both sole adversary and sole arbiter is precisely the single point of correlated failure that multiplicity exists to remove. What the rebuttal *uniquely* produces is the author's response under attack — update or defend, with named reasons. That response is the load-bearing output; it exists only if the author speaks again.
- *One* confrontation round — derivable, not chosen: zero rounds = no premise ever exposed; one round = every stated premise exposed to an adversary at least once; more rounds = unbounded cost with diminishing returns. One is the fixed point of "expose everything once, at minimum cost."
- *Synthesis with attribution* — one judge, full transcript, forced to either find no ugliness (approve with grafted framing) or name the essence-level disagreement (converge as terminal handoff).

**3. Three seats, derived rather than asserted.** The six BEAUTY GATE smells factor into exactly three families: **purpose** (skipped teleology, missing inevitability), **economy** (magic numbers, symptom branches), **truth** (proxy patches, narrative-driven). One seat per family gives full-rubric coverage with maximal inter-seat orthogonality; a fourth same-family seat buys correlation, not decorrelation — the real fourth perspective is the cross-model oracle, already standing doctrine. If 3 is to survive my own lens, it must be this derivation and not a number with a story. (The gate-mode high-risk seat survives separately because prompt injection is not an aesthetic question; it is a different threat model.)

**4. A sentinel diet.** Sentinel status is earned by a *machine branch*, nothing else. VERDICT routes; STANCE is the machine-checkable unanimity re-check after confrontation; GAP feeds gate-mode reject handling; REPLY anchors the fail-closed adjacency parse. ESSENCE and WEAKEST are prose discipline — mandate them as required sections in the prompt, but do not fail-closed-parse them: the existing parser's cost model (any malformed pair ⇒ nil ⇒ the whole spawn wasted, core.lua:396-474) means every ceremonial sentinel converts an essay-shape defect into minutes of burned frontier compute.

**5. The deletion manifest is part of the design, same PR** (改契约就改完整; one canonical way, bypass forbidden):
- `prior_round_digests`: field, validator, `max_prior_round_digests`, and the builder write (builders.lua:364) — dead today, purposeless tomorrow.
- The digest-based deliberation channel and its Reply/Digest duplication — digests may survive only as outer-surface telemetry, never as what any judge reads.
- The `⟦FKST:PLAN⟧` outcome (prompts/meta_judge.lua:12-14): a hedge state — "compatible but need another round on the merged framing" — made redundant by a synthesis judge empowered to graft the merged framing and approve it. Three outcome kinds collapse to two: reached, converge.
- The round cap **re-derived, not inherited**: 8 was priced for 4-spawn amnesiac rounds (worst case 32 spawns); at 7 spawns per disagreement round it prices a 56-spawn worst case nobody chose. Under a debate that either resolves or names its disagreement, more than 2–3 outer rounds is evidence of a defect, not a budget.
- *(Ambient exhibit of how ceremony accretes unnoticed: `published_seam = { "proposal" }` is declared twice in the same spec table, decide/main.lua:6 and :8 — harmless, unread, and still there.)*

**6. Crash-only, no checkpoints.** A crash mid-debate re-runs the whole debate; dedup stays at the decision boundary (main.lua:164-173). The retry quantum grows from 4 to 7 spawns; accepting that is cheaper and more honest than adding per-phase durable state, which would be a second truth source. This is the one place I *refuse* a deletion-adjacent economy (checkpointing to "save" spawns) because it buys state, and state is the most expensive thing in this system.

### CRITIQUE

**Magic numbers.** The draft keeps the outer cap "~8 unchanged" while tripling the cost of a disagreement round — a constant surviving its justification is the definition of this smell. "Three philosophers" is asserted, never derived; without the six-smells-factor-into-three-families derivation (or an equivalent), 3 is numerology. And wherever a 600-character digest remains on any path a judge reads, the draft has only half-deleted the truncation it correctly diagnosed.

**Proxy patches.** Full credit: the draft attacks the biggest proxy in the system — verdict-agreement over 600-char truncations standing in for confronted objections — and its fix (full P1 text in-memory for rebuttal and synthesis) is a genuine deletion of a truncation, in the spirit of the G-CONTENT-TRUNCATION ratchet. But it retains `narrowed_question` as the inter-round channel without committing to the consequence: if the inner debate works, that one line stops being a *working* channel and becomes a *terminal label* on a named disagreement, and outer-round recurrence should be near zero. A draft that fixes the inner channel while leaving the outer channel's deliberative role intact has fixed half of one amnesia.

**Symptom branches.** PLAN is a hedge state that the draft's own synthesis judge makes unrepresentable-by-need — yet the draft's phase-3 contract lists it as a live outcome. Likewise `default_narrowed_question` (core.lua:650-661): under the old economics a synthesis parse failure costing one cheap blind re-round was tolerable; under the new economics that fallback burns a 7-spawn round to recover from one malformed line. Failure of the synthesis step should be retried at the width of one spawn, not one debate.

**Narrative-driven.** The cost story — "7 per round vs today's 4 × up to 8" — is favorable framing, not accounting: the honest comparison is against the *distribution* of today's rounds, plus a stated expectation for outer-round recurrence under the new design. Separately, the claim that three seats sharing the *full* BEAUTY GATE rubric with different "primary lens" seeds decorrelate better than today's crude but genuinely disjoint biases is an unverified story. Three judges reading the same six-smell rubric with different bold lines may be three samples of one judge — count without decorrelation is pure cost. The spec must require genuinely disjoint lens texts, or the philosopher's court is a choir.

**Missing inevitability.** The draft's skeleton — blind → conditional confrontation → synthesis — is, I believe, very close to the inevitable shape (my IDEAL derives essentially the same one). But the draft *asserts* it: 3 seats, 2 phases, 4 new sentinels, listed as choices. Every one of those has a derivation available (§IDEAL 2-4); until they are written in, the draft reads as "one plausible design among several," which is this gate's named failure.

**Skipped teleology.** What is the rebuttal phase *for*? If it is for machine-checkable convergence-after-confrontation, then ⟦STANCE⟧ is the single load-bearing new sentinel and must be fail-closed — while ⟦ESSENCE⟧ and ⟦WEAKEST⟧, which no machine branches on, must be demoted to mandated prose sections before their parse failures start billing us by the spawn. The draft treats all new sentinels as peers; teleology says they are not. Two further unasked purpose-questions: does the 4th (high-risk) gate seat join the rebuttal — unspecified, therefore unbounded surface; and does gate mode (PR review: evidence adequacy under a threat model) want a beauty debate *at all*, or only the essence-independence of blind positions? The draft grafts the machinery onto both modes without asking either question.

**The overarching finding: an addition disguised as a replacement.** The standing proof of this repo's canonical failure mode is already in the tree: `prior_round_digests`, added to carry inter-round memory, validated forever (core.lua:304), consumed never. The draft — whose entire moral claim is *fidelity to essence* — names not one deletion: not the digest channel, not the Reply/Digest duplication, not PLAN, not the dead field, not the re-derivation of the cap. A spec that does not name its deletions will be implemented as an addition; that is not a prediction, it is a grep away from being history repeating.

### VERDICT

abstain

### WEAKEST

My derivation of "exactly 3 seats from the six-smell factorization" may itself be post-hoc numerology — the six smells are neither proven exhaustive nor independent, so the seat count could legitimately be a free parameter, which would undercut my sharpest anti-magic-number charge.

## Seat 3: FIDELITY (blind) — verdict: abstain

Verified preface: the current angle contract is a two-line verdict+reply judgment with one bias line (`prompts/angle.lua:2-29`); the flow is 3 parallel peer-invisible angles → unanimity fast-path → sync meta-judge (`departments/decide/main.lua:59-126`); the meta-judge today sees only 600-char digests, not full angle text (`core/prompt_rendering.lua:52-65`, `core.lua:565-589`); a convergence round shows returning workers exactly one `Convergence question:` line (`core/prompt_rendering.lua:88-91`); `prior_round_digests` is shipped by the upstream loop (asserted in `packages/github-devloop/tests/core_flow_test.lua:788-809`, with a comment promising "so the next angles" can see it), validated by consensus (`core.lua:234-247, 304`), and rendered into no prompt anywhere (grep across the package: validation and tests only); round cap is 8 (`libraries/devloop/config.lua:135-137`); the BEAUTY GATE section of CLAUDE.md explicitly states same-model adversarial review has correlated blindness and mandates cross-model-family + human backstop as its third layer.

### ESSENCE

Strip away the theater of "three grandmaster philosophers" and ask what this machine is *for*. The consensus engine is an instrument that measures a latent variable — the fidelity of a proposal to the essence of its problem — using noisy, **correlated** sensors, and emits an irreversible verdict. Every design question about it reduces to one: **does this mechanism increase the information the final verdict carries about the latent variable, measured against a ground truth the system can eventually observe — or does it increase the persuasiveness of the transcript?** Those are different quantities, and for LLM judges they routinely anticorrelate, because fluent narrative is the one thing an LLM produces at zero marginal cost.

Three consequences follow from first principles:

1. **A signal is worth what it costs to fake.** Condorcet's jury theorem — the entire mathematical case for taking a vote of three judges instead of one — holds only for *independent* errors. Three samples from the same model family, primed with the same doctrine text, are closer to one measurement re-run three times. Verdict words, stance declarations, self-named weakest assumptions: all free to generate, therefore all zero-information unless something external makes them expensive. What is expensive to fake: a citation to a file:line that contradicts a premise (checkable now), and a prediction about post-merge reality (checkable later). A debate design is faithful to its essence exactly insofar as it routes conviction through those two currencies.

2. **The costly error is asymmetric.** A false converge burns rounds and reconciles to `blocked` — bounded, visible, recoverable. A false approve merges plausible-but-wrong code and is invisible to both the safety and liveness nets; the repo's own competence axis names this the deepest blind spot. So the design must be judged principally on whether adversarial structure reduces false-approve — an empirical claim, not an aesthetic one.

3. **An instrument that never touches its calibration standard is not an instrument.** The repo already names the ground truth: `false_consensus_rate`, revert/reopen within N days, the AVM ledger. A debate engine whose accuracy claims cannot be joined against that ledger is decoration, however beautiful its transcripts.

### IDEAL

**Decorrelate by construction, not exhortation.** You cannot instruct independence into three copies of one prior. Real levers, strongest first: (a) *cross-model-family seats* — the constitution already mandates this in the BEAUTY GATE itself ("对抗也会 correlated 失明…ChatGPT Pro 作跨模型族对抗") and the `nyxid oracle` channel already exists as standing infrastructure; (b) *evidence partition* — seat the philosophers on different slices of ground truth (one reads the diff, one reads only the tests, one reads only the stated goal and the issue history), so that where the artifact is internally inconsistent, they are *forced* into genuine disagreement by their inputs rather than invited into performed disagreement by their prompts; (c) *lock positions before exposure to the proposal's own rhetoric* — essence derived from `source_ref` facts before reading the author's argument, so one confident narrative cannot anchor all three sensors at once.

**Make debate moves out of checkable claims.** The rebuttal contract is not "attack the peer's ESSENCE" — one narrative attacking another is narrative squared. It is: *name a fact which, if verified, breaks the peer's position, with provenance* (file:line from the context manifest, a command output, a marker). A STANCE change must cite the specific fact-claim that moved it. The synthesis judge's job is then not to weigh eloquence but to *check the load-bearing citations* — it holds the same manifest and can read the same files. "The debate actually bit" becomes machine-verifiable from the record: count verified fact-claims that changed a verdict. Zero such claims across a debate = the transcript is theater, and the engine can *say so* in its own output.

**Every approve is a wager, and the house keeps score.** Stamp each reached verdict with its provenance path — `blind-unanimity | post-rebuttal-unanimity | synthesis` — plus who dissented and folded. Join that against reverts/reopens/fix-forwards. Then "unanimity after rebuttal is stronger than blind unanimity" stops being a founding myth and becomes a row in a table that can prove the architecture wrong. The debate engine submits to the same instrument it applies to proposals. This is the one feature that makes every other feature honest.

**Nothing self-certifies.** ESSENCE, WEAKEST, smell-audits are 留痕 — attack surface for peers, never gate inputs. This is the constitution's own hardest-won line (自评丑必被作弊), purchased with incident #1195. The fail-closed sentinel parse layer stays; any structured field no machine ever reads is either wired up or deleted.

**The cheap path survives.** Blind round one, escalate only on real disagreement — the same bounded-escalation shape as the core loop. This the draft has right, and it is right *because* blind unanimity, being uncoordinated, is the one cheap signal that is not manufactured.

### CRITIQUE

**1. Proxy patch (central): "beauty under adversarial scrutiny" measures agreement-after-instructed-attack among same-family workers, a proxy for adversarial scrutiny.** All seven spawns in the draft are codex. The repo's own BEAUTY GATE states, verbatim, that same-model adversarial review suffers correlated blindness and installs cross-model-family opposition plus human backstop as its third layer — the layer this draft silently drops while borrowing the gate's vocabulary for its approve semantics ("no ugliness found under adversarial scrutiny"). ## Seat 3: FIDELITY (rebuttal) — final verdict: approve

### ATTACK ON PEER A (Teleology) — the root

**Weakest premise: Corollary 2 — "a rebuttal is a conditional sample given the peer's argument; only the second can break a correlated error."** The premise treats the conditional sample's effect as if its sign were known. It is not. Conditioning one same-family instance on another's *narrative* is, with equal mechanical plausibility, the mechanism of **cascade**: the confident wrong story propagates into the peer's context and *increases* correlation. This repo's constitution states the failure form exactly: feeding an unverified premise downstream "= 制造 correlated 幻觉" (manufacturing correlated hallucinations), and its incident record shows it happening — the false-oracle-premise incident where a narrative was amplified into "一整套精致错设计" and filed. Prior art agrees on the condition under which the sign flips positive: debate-as-oracle protocols (Irving et al.) work because debaters are *zero-sum adversaries* and the judge verifies refutations against **ground-truth elements the debaters can point at** — not because arguments merely meet. Delphi works through *facilitated, structured* feedback, not raw confrontation. Nemeth's dissent research distinguishes authentic from assigned dissent by *incentive*, which three cooperative codex instances do not have.

So the root correction: **confrontation breaks correlated error if and only if the confrontation currency is verifiable against ground truth external to both narrators; otherwise conditioning is unsigned and may homogenize.** Peer A's own text contains the missing half-sentence — "the judge can verify a refutation far more cheaply than it can verify a claim" — but never asks *against what* the judge verifies. A refutation of a narrative by a narrative is not cheap to verify; it is impossible to verify. A refutation via a named fact (file:line in the already-fetched manifest) is cheap. The telos derivation stops one step short of deriving the currency, and everything downstream (rebuttal contract, findings record) inherits that gap.

**Secondary root inconsistency:** Peer A's essence demands "independent epistemic evidence that **no single model instance can manufacture alone**" — and then seats three instances of the same model and never mentions the cross-model oracle once. Three copies of one prior satisfy that clause only trivially. The constitution's BEAUTY GATE names cross-family opposition as its third layer *because* same-family adversarial review has correlated blindness (#1195 was caught by the human, not the triplet). Peer A's ideal quietly deletes the one seat its own essence sentence requires. I do not demand the oracle in the verdict path (see below — I have updated there); I demand that a teleology paper not derive independence and then omit its only available source.

What survives the attack: corollaries 1 and 3 stand. The critique of `prompts/angle.lua:4` (whole-picture judgment forbidden by contract) is the single sharpest observation in either paper, and I could not break it.
